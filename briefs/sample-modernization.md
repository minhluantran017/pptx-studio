# Sample brief: Cloud platform modernization

## Audience
Executive sponsors and engineering leaders evaluating a 12-month cloud platform program.

## Purpose
Gain approval for a four-week discovery and an initial shared-platform investment gate.

## Key message
Standardize the secure foundation, migrate in measured waves, and release further funding when outcome measures support it.

## Required slides
Cover, executive summary, target architecture, delivery roadmap, success measures, recommendation.

## Data note
All targets in this sample are illustrative planning assumptions, not measured results or commitments.
